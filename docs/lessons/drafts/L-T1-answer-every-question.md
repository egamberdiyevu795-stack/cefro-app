## 2. Explanation

In Part 2 you get three questions and two minutes. Every question you leave out lowers your band, however good your
English is. If you answer two questions out of three, the highest band you can get is 3.

Use your minute of preparation to write one short note for each question. Then speak in the same order: Question 1,
Question 2, Question 3. Start each answer with a sentence that clearly answers that question, so the listener knows
where you are. Watch the time. After about 40 seconds on one question, move on to the next one. A short answer to
every question is better than a long answer to one.

*(112 words)*
