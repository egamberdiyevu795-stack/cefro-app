## 2. Explanation

In Part 2, one bare sentence does not count as an answer. A bare answer like "Yes, I did." or "No, they shouldn't." is marked as not answered. An answer that only touches the question counts, but it shows very little.

Build every answer in three steps:

1. **Answer:** answer the question directly in your first sentence.
2. **Reason:** say why, with *because*, *since* or *so*.
3. **Example:** add one detail from your life: when, where, what happened.

That takes about 30 seconds per question, which fits two minutes. It also lets you show B2 language: linking words,
precise vocabulary and longer sentences. Short on time? Keep the answer and the reason.

*(110 words)*

## 3. Examples

Example topic (made up, **not** the retry task): *"Describe a time when someone helped you."*
Q1 Who helped you, and what situation were you in? · Q2 How did they help you, and how did you feel? ·
Q3 Do you think people help each other less today than in the past? Why or why not?
These examples use a different topic from your task, so you practise the skill, not the words.

| # | Problem | Weaker | Better | Note | Made up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Question 1, bare sentence (`minimal`) | "My friend helped me." | "My best friend helped me last spring, when my laptop broke two days before a big project was due. I had lost almost all my work and didn't know what to do." | Answer (who) + situation + a detail of what was happening. | yes |
| 2 | Question 2, bare sentence (`minimal`) | "He lent me his laptop, and I was happy." | "He lent me his own laptop for the whole weekend, even though he needed it himself, so I could finish on time. I was so grateful that I bought him dinner afterwards." | Answer + reason (*so I could…*) + what happened next. | yes |
| 3 | Question 3, bare sentence (`minimal`) | "Yes, less." | "Yes, I think people help each other less than in the past. Life is busier now, so people have less time for their neighbours. For example, my grandparents knew everyone in their street, while I hardly know anyone in my building." | Answer + reason + example: the full three steps. | yes |
| 4 | Question 3, touched on but not answered (`partial`) | "People are very busy these days." | "On the whole, I'd say people help each other less face to face, but more in other ways. Everyone is busy, so we rarely visit neighbours, but many people help strangers online, for example by answering questions in forums." | The question asks "do you think…?", so say yes, no or "it depends" first, then the reason. | yes |

## 4. Speaking drill

Record each prompt, listen back, then tick the checklist. Self-check only, no AI scoring.

Drill set version 2: the neutral drill from `docs/lessons/drill-drafts.md`, approved by Asadbek on 2026-11-07. The same drill is shown for every retry task.

Drill topic (made up): *"Describe a book or film you enjoyed."*
Q1 What was it, and when did you read or watch it? · Q2 What was it about, and why did you enjoy it? ·
Q3 Is reading books still important for young people today? Why or why not?

| # | Prompt | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What was the book or film, and when did you read or watch it? Answer in one sentence, then add one detail. | 20 s |
| 2 | Is reading books still important for young people today? Answer, give a reason, then start "For example, …". | 30 s |
| 3 | Make this bare answer full: "Yes, I enjoyed it." Add a reason and one detail from the story. | 20 s |

**Checklist**

- [ ] My first sentence answered the question directly.
- [ ] I added a reason (*because*, *since*, *so*).
- [ ] I added an example or a detail from my own life.
