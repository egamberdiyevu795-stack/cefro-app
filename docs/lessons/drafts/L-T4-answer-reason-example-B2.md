## 1. Why this lesson

Shown at the top, filled in from the result:

> Your answer to Question {n} was too short to count: "{evidence quote}". {evaluator note}

(For a `partial` answer: "Your answer to Question {n} only touched on the question: "{evidence quote}".")

## 2. Explanation

In Part 2, one bare sentence does not count as an answer. "My father gave me advice." or "No, they shouldn't." is
marked as not answered. An answer that only touches the question counts, but it shows very little.

Build every answer in three steps:

1. **Answer:** answer the question directly in your first sentence.
2. **Reason:** say why, with *because*, *since* or *so*.
3. **Example:** add one detail from your life: when, where, what happened.

That takes about 30 seconds per question, which fits two minutes. It also lets you show B2 language: linking words,
precise vocabulary and longer sentences. Short on time? Keep the answer and the reason.

*(110 words)*

## 3. Examples

| 1 | Question 1, bare sentence (`minimal`) | "My teacher gave me advice." | "My English teacher gave me some advice in my last year at school, when I was panicking about my exams. | 4 | Question 3, touched on but not answered (`partial`) | "Older people know a lot of things about life." | "On the whole, I think young people should listen to older people, but not blindly. They know a lot about life, so their advice is worth hearing, but in the end we have to make our own decisions." | The question asks "should…?", so say yes, no or "it depends" first, then the reason. | yes |

## 4. Speaking drill

Record each prompt, listen back, then tick the checklist. Self-check only, no AI scoring.

| # | Prompt | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Who gave you advice, and what situation were you in? Answer in one sentence, then add one detail. | 20 s |
| 2 | What was the advice, and did you follow it? Answer, then add "because…". | 20 s |
| 3 | Should young people always follow advice from older people? Answer, give a reason, then start "For example, …". | 30 s |
| 4 | Make this bare answer full: "Yes, I followed it." Add a reason and what happened next. | 20 s |
| 5 | Make this answer direct: "Older people have a lot of experience." Start with your yes / no / it depends. | 20 s |

**Checklist**

- [ ] My first sentence answered the question directly.
- [ ] I added a reason (*because*, *since*, *so*).
- [ ] I added an example or a detail from my own life.

## 5. Retry the same task

Same task, same timings: 1 minute preparation, 2 minutes speaking. Scored by the AI as a normal retry.

- **Objective:** Answer question {n} fully: "{question text}"
- **Success check:** You answer question {n} directly in your first sentence, then add a reason or an example.
- **Lesson reminder on the retry screen:** For every question: answer → reason → example.

(The objective and success check use the same wording as the existing retry focus for a `partial` or `minimal`
