## 1. Why this lesson

Shown at the top, filled in from the result:

> Your answer described the picture, but it did not answer the three questions. {evaluator note}

## 2. Explanation

The picture in Part 2 only shows the topic. It is not a question, and describing it scores nothing. "The man is
giving advice to the girl" answers no question, even though it uses the word "advice". An answer that only describes
the picture gets band 0.

Read the three questions, not the picture. Questions 1 and 2 ask about a time when **you** received advice: your own
story. Question 3 asks for **your** opinion. If no story comes quickly, use a simple true one: a teacher, a parent or
a friend who told you what to do. You can look at the picture for an idea, but then talk about yourself.

## 4. Speaking drill

Record each prompt, listen back, then tick the checklist. Self-check only, no AI scoring.

| # | Prompt | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Without looking at the picture, say who gave you advice and what situation you were in. | 20 s |
| 2 | Say what the advice was and whether you followed it. | 20 s |
| 3 | Give your opinion: should young people always follow advice from older people? Give one reason. | 30 s |
| 4 | Start with "This picture reminds me of…" and move into your own story within one sentence. | 20 s |

**Checklist**

- [ ] I talked about my own experience, not the people in the picture.
