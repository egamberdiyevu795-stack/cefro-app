**Approved by Asadbek on 2026-11-07 and in use.** These drills replace the lessons' first drills (written for
"Advice from someone", now retired) in all three lesson files and in the app, word for word (tested), and the same drill
is shown for every retry task. A test checks that no prompt uses a word from any of the 7 Part 2 tasks' topics.

Why: the first drills were on "Advice from someone". A neutral drill topic avoids practising on one task's topic
before retrying another. Each set below uses its own made-up topic, which is **not** one of the 7 Part 2
tasks (advice, an important decision, a goal, working in a team, trying and failing, a tradition, a happy day) and
**not** the lessons' example topic ("someone helped you"). All topics are everyday and safe for teenagers.

The drill instruction and the checklist stay as in each lesson.

## L-T1 — Answer every question (neutral drill)

Drill topic (made up): *"Describe a place you like to visit."*
Q1 Where is the place, and how often do you go there? · Q2 What do you do there, and why do you like it? ·
Q3 Do you think young people spend enough time outdoors? Why or why not?

| # | Prompt | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Where is the place, and how often do you go there? Answer, then add one detail (when, who with or why). | 20 s |
| 2 | What do you do there, and why do you like it? Answer, then add one reason or what happened last time you went. | 20 s |
| 3 | **Planning drill.** You have 60 seconds, as in the preparation minute. Say your three notes aloud, one for each question (place + how often / what you do + why / opinion + reason). | 60 s |

## L-T8 — The picture is not the question (neutral drill)

Drill topic (made up): *"Describe a skill you learned outside school."*
Q1 What was the skill, and who taught you? · Q2 How long did it take, and how did you feel when you could do it? ·
Q3 Is it better to learn new skills from a person or from videos online? Why?
Drill picture (made up): a boy learning to ride a bicycle in a park while his older sister holds the seat.

| # | Prompt | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Without looking at the picture, say what skill you learned and who taught you. | 20 s |
| 2 | Give your opinion: is it better to learn new skills from a person or from videos online? Give one reason. | 30 s |
| 3 | Start with "This picture reminds me of…" and move into your own story within one sentence. | 20 s |

## L-T4 (B2) — Answer, reason, example (neutral drill)

Drill topic (made up): *"Describe a book or film you enjoyed."*
Q1 What was it, and when did you read or watch it? · Q2 What was it about, and why did you enjoy it? ·
Q3 Is reading books still important for young people today? Why or why not?

| # | Prompt | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What was the book or film, and when did you read or watch it? Answer in one sentence, then add one detail. | 20 s |
| 2 | Is reading books still important for young people today? Answer, give a reason, then start "For example, …". | 30 s |
| 3 | Make this bare answer full: "Yes, I enjoyed it." Add a reason and one detail from the story. | 20 s |
