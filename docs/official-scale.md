# Official UZBMB scale check (multilevel speaking)

Status: **partial, 2026-11-11.** Step 3 of `docs/v1-plan.md`'s build order. This file is documents only: no app code,
no migration, no model call.

**Short answer:** lex.uz could be read, and it gives the legal frame plus one score boundary (B2 = at least 51
points). **uzbmb.uz could not be read properly from the sandbox**, and UZBMB's own website is where the law says the
score-to-level table is published. So the full table, the speaking rubric and the rule that combines speaking with the
other skills are **not confirmed here**. The mock result page must not show a score or claim the official conversion
until someone reads the uzbmb.uz documents listed in section 4.

## 1. Officially published (read and quoted from the source)

All quotes are from lex.uz (the Ministry of Justice's national legal database), read on 2026-11-11 in the current
edition shown there. Translations are ours.

### 1.1 Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 73 of 16.02.2022 (in force; last amended 30.07.2026)

Source: <https://lex.uz/docs/-5863626>. Title: "Chet tilini bilish darajasini aniqlash boʻyicha davlat xizmatlari
koʻrsatishning maʼmuriy reglamentini tasdiqlash toʻgʻrisida" ("On approving the administrative regulation for the
state service of determining foreign-language proficiency"). The quotes below are from its Annex 1 unless stated.

**The multilevel test exists, and it covers B1, B2 and C1** (resolution, item 3, wording of Resolution No. 17 of
10.01.2024):

> "…umumlashtirilgan koʻp darajali (Multi-level) test tizimini (B1, B2, C1) joriy qilish toʻgʻrisidagi taklifi
> maʼqullansin."
>
> "…the proposal to introduce a generalised multi-level (Multi-level) test system (B1, B2, C1) is approved."

> "…koʻp darajali (Multi-level) formati 2022-yil 1-martdan boshlab — ingliz tili uchun… joriy etiladi."
>
> "…the multi-level (Multi-level) format is introduced from 1 March 2022 for English…"

**Multilevel results are given by a table, not by the 60 % rule** (item 5):

> "Malaka sertifikati yoki maʼlumotnoma malaka imtihoni yakunlari boʻyicha eng yuqori ballning kamida 60 foizini
> toʻplaganda yoki koʻp darajali test sinovlari yakunlari natijalarini belgilovchi jadval asosidagi ballar boʻyicha
> beriladi."
>
> "The qualification certificate or reference is issued when at least 60 % of the maximum score of the qualification
> exam is gained, **or according to the scores in the table that sets the results of multi-level tests**."

**Who writes the table and where it is published** (item 6):

> "Vakolatli organ tomonidan koʻp darajali test topshiriqlarining qiyinlik darajasi boʻyicha malaka imtihoni
> natijalarini belgilovchi jadval ishlab chiqiladi va ilmiy-metodik kengash tomonidan tasdiqlanadi hamda vakolatli
> organning rasmiy veb-saytida eʼlon qilinadi."
>
> "The competent body [UZBMB] develops the table that sets qualification exam results by the difficulty level of the
> multi-level test tasks; it is approved by the scientific-methodological council and **published on the competent
> body's official website**."

> "Koʻp darajali format boʻyicha test natijalari qiyinlik darajasi boʻyicha belgilangan malaka imtihon natijalarini
> belgilovchi jadvalga muvofiq eʼlon qilinadi." (item 31)
>
> "Test results in the multi-level format are announced according to the table that sets qualification exam results
> by difficulty level."

**The speaking section: what it tests, how long, how many tasks, who marks it**:

> "“gapirish” boʻlimi — tegishli darajadagi grammatik tuzilishdan va lugʻat zaxirasidan foydalangan holda, berilgan
> savollarga mos javob berish va oʻz nuqtai nazarini asoslash mahoratini tekshiradi." (item 17 g)
>
> "The 'speaking' section tests the ability to answer the given questions appropriately and to justify one's own point
> of view, using grammar and vocabulary of the relevant level."

> "koʻp darajali format boʻyicha — … “gapirish” boʻlimi uchun — 15 daqiqa" (item 25)
>
> "for the multi-level format — … for the 'speaking' section — 15 minutes"

> "27. Koʻp darajali test sinovi: … “gapirish” boʻlimi topshiriqlarning 3 ta turidan iborat boʻladi." (item 27)
>
> "27. The multi-level test: … the 'speaking' section consists of 3 types of tasks."

> "“Yozma ish” va “gapirish” boʻlimlari boʻyicha bilimlar maxsus dasturlar yordamida imtihon komissiyasi tomonidan
> tekshiriladi va natijalari rasmiylashtiriladi." (item 24)
>
> "Knowledge in the 'writing' and 'speaking' sections is checked by the exam commission with the help of special
> software, and the results are recorded."

**Points: 30 per section and 120 in total, but see the note** (item 31):

> "Test sinovi har bir boʻlimining eng yuqori bahosi 30 ballni va barcha boʻlimlar uchun maksimal baho 120 ballni
> tashkil etadi. Test sinovi yakunlari boʻyicha eng yuqori ballning 60 foizidan kam (72 balldan kam) ball olgan
> talabgor daraja shartlarini bajarmagan deb hisoblanadi."
>
> "The highest mark for each section of the test is 30 points, and the maximum for all sections is 120 points. A
> candidate who scores less than 60 % of the maximum (less than 72 points) is considered not to have met the level
> requirements."

Note: these figures sit next to the 60 % rule, which item 5 applies to the **single-level** exams. The very next
sentence of item 31 then says the multilevel results follow the table. The text does not say whether "30 per
section" also applies to the multilevel format. Section 3 lists this as unknown.

**The certificate shows one score per skill, an overall score and a level** (Annex 3, the certificate template).
The fields are: "Daraja | Level", "Tinglab tushunish | Listening", "Oʻqish | Reading", "Yozish | Writing",
"Gapirish | Speaking", "Umumiy ball | Overall Score". The template does not say how the overall score is worked out.

### 1.2 Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 143 of 06.04.2023 (Law Enforcement Academy admissions)

Source: <https://lex.uz/docs/-6425532>. In the regulation it approves, item 61 lists which certificates earn the
maximum foreign-language mark without an exam:

> "ingliz tili uchun — B2 (“Multilevel” test sinovida kamida 51 ball) va undan yuqori darajadagi milliy sertifikat…"
>
> "for English — a national certificate of level B2 (**at least 51 points in the 'Multilevel' test**) or higher…"

This is the only **numeric** level boundary of the multilevel test found in an official source we could read. It is
about the **overall** multilevel score, not the speaking score. The maximum of that scale is not stated there.

### 1.3 Repealed: Resolution No. 352 of 31.12.2013 (context only)

Source: <https://lex.uz/docs/-2314783>. lex.uz marks it "Hujjat kuchini yo‘qotgan 01.03.2022" ("The document lost
force on 01.03.2022"); Resolution No. 73 (Annex 2, point 1) repealed it. It described the old single-level exams
(30 points per section, 150 in total, 60 % to pass). **Do not use it for the multilevel exam.**

### 1.4 UZBMB's own documents (read 2026-11-11 from files downloaded by Asadbek)

**A. "Chet tilini bilish darajasini baholash ko‘p darajali test formati uchun baholash mezonlari"**
(`https://uzbmb.uz/upload/file/pdf/Multilevel-bm.pdf`; approved by UZBMB's Scientific-Methodological Council on
16.03.2023, applied from the March 2023 exams).

1. **Every skill is on a 0–75 scale.** Listening and reading: Rasch ability → standard score `T = Z × 10 + 50`, capped
   at 75 "because no C2 tasks exist" (all results at +2.5 and above count as 75). Writing and speaking: "Yozma ish va
   gapirish bo‘limlarining har birida maksimal ball 75 balldan iborat." ("Writing and speaking each have a maximum of
   75 points.")
2. **Speaking gets one combined mark over its three task types:** "Gapirish ko‘nikmasida esa topshiriqlarning 3 turi
   bo‘yicha umumlashgan baho qo‘yiladi." ("In speaking, a combined mark is given across the 3 task types.")
3. **Experts' marks are averaged, then converted with a table** ("Ekspertlar tomonidan qo‘yilgan ballarning o‘rta
   arifmetigi quyidagi shkala bo‘yicha yakuniy bahoga aylantiriladi"). The table printed there runs from raw
   **0–36.0 → 0–75** (e.g. 20.6–21.0 → 51, 28.1–29.0 → 65, 36 → 75). Raw 36 is writing's maximum (task 1 = 12,
   task 2 = 24), so this table fits **writing**; it does not fit the new speaking rubric below (maximum 21), and
   applying it to speaking would be our guess.
4. **The level comes from the average of the four skills:** "testning 4 bo‘limi bo‘yicha o‘rtacha ball hisoblanadi va
   yakuniy natijalar quyidagi shkala bo‘yicha e’lon qilinadi" ("the average score of the 4 sections is calculated and
   the final results are announced on this scale"):

   | Daraja (level) | Umumiy ball (score) |
   | --- | --- |
   | C1 | 65–75 |
   | B2 | 51–64 |
   | B1 | 38–50 |
   | B1dan quyi (below B1) | 0–37 |

   This matches the one boundary confirmed on lex.uz (B2 = at least 51, section 1.2).

**B. "Ko‘p darajali (multilevel) imtihonlarning gapirish bo‘limi uchun baholash mezonlari" — new format**
(`https://uzbmb.uz/upload/file/pdf/phone/yangi_format_uzbek_rus.pdf`, Uzbek and Russian).

5. **Four marks, one per question group:** Questions 1–3 **0–5** (5 = "above A2"), Questions 4–6 **0–5** (5 = "above
   B1"), Question 7 **0–5** (5 = "above B2"), Question 8 **0–6** (5 = C1, 6 = "above C1"). So the raw speaking
   maximum is **5 + 5 + 5 + 6 = 21**.
6. **This is exactly our structure:** Part 1.1 = Q1–3, Part 1.2 = Q4–6, Part 2 = Q7, Part 3 = Q8, with the same band
   ranges (our rubrics `multilevel_q1_3`, `multilevel_q4_6`, `multilevel_q7`, `multilevel_q8`). Nothing in the exam
   format changed that our app must follow.
7. **Memorised answers score 0:** band 0 includes "barcha javoblar mavzudan butunlay tashqarida (masalan, yodlangan
   javoblar, taxminlar)" ("all answers fully off topic, e.g. memorised answers, guesses"). This supports V1 decision
   Q2 (no rewritten model answers).

**C. "C1 Gapirish ko‘nikmasini baholash mezoni"** (`C1_baholash(gap).pdf`): a 0–6 grid on five criteria (vocabulary,
grammar, fluency and coherence, communicative effectiveness, pronunciation) for the **single-level C1** exam, not the
multilevel test. Not used for the mock level.

**Still missing:** the official conversion of the new raw speaking total (0–21) to the 0–75 speaking score. The
uzbmb.uz speaking page (screenshot, 11.11.26) lists it as a separate document, "Ko‘p darajali format gapirish
ko‘nikmasini baholash reyting shkalasi" ("rating scale for multilevel speaking"), next to "Gapirish ko‘nikmasini
baholashning yangi formati".

## 5. Recommendation for the mock result page (step 4) — updated 2026-11-11

**The official path, used as far as it is published:**

1. Our four part bands are the official four speaking marks (section 1.4, item 6). Their sum is the official raw
   speaking total, **0–21**.
2. **Raw 0–21 → speaking score 0–75: official table still missing** (section 1.4, "Still missing"). Until it is read,
   the app does **not** convert and shows **no score**.
3. **Score → level: official** (section 1.4, item 4): C1 65–75, B2 51–64, B1 38–50, below B1 0–37. Once step 2 is
   known, the mock shows "Speaking: 58 / 75 · B2 (estimate)" through this table, all in one swappable function.

**Until the conversion is read, step 4 keeps the interim rule below** (ours, in one pure function, swapped for
"sum → official conversion → official level table" when the rating scale is read):

| Estimated level | When |
| --- | --- |
| C1 | Part 3 band ≥ 5 ("C1" or "Above C1") **and** Part 2 band ≥ 3 (B2 or above) |
| B2 | Part 2 band ≥ 3 ("Lower B2" or above), or Part 3 band 3–4 (B2) |
| B1 | Part 2 band 1–2 (B1), or Part 1.2 band ≥ 3 ("Lower B1" or above) |
| Below B1 | None of the above |

If a part has no AI band yet ("AI score not ready yet"), show no overall estimate and say "Estimate when all parts are
scored". The teacher's level (V1 Q6) sits beside it.

**The line under the level:**

> **Speaking only. An estimate from this mock, not an official UZBMB score.**

It stays even after the official conversion is used: the level is still our AI's marks, not an examiner's, and the
certificate level averages four skills. Uzbek and Russian versions need a native check before the pilot:

- Uzbek (draft): "Faqat gapirish. Bu mock natijasiga koʻra taxminiy daraja, UZBMB rasmiy bali emas."
- Russian (draft): "Только говорение. Оценка по этому пробному экзамену, а не официальный балл UZBMB."

**Do not show:** "Passed" / "Failed", "Certificate level", or any 0–75 number before the official raw-to-75 table is
read.

## Update 2026-11-13: the raw 0–21 → 0–75 speaking table is in, and the interim rule is replaced

The conversion that was "still missing" (section 1.4, "Still missing"; section 5, step 2; section 6) is now known.

- **Source:** UZBMB, "Ko‘p darajali format gapirish ko‘nikmasini baholash reyting shkalasi" (rating scale for multilevel speaking),
  <https://uzbmb.uz/upload/file/pdf/phone/reyting_shkala.pdf>, listed on <https://gov.uz/oz/uzbmb/sections/view/49531>.
  Supplied by the project owner as text and checked 2026-11-13. In code: `src/domain/speaking/speaking-score-table.ts` (the only place
  the values live, with this source), `rawToSpeaking75` in `speaking-score.ts`.
- **Raw = the sum of the four marks** (Q1–3 + Q4–6 + Q7 + Q8 = our Part 1.1 + 1.2 + 2 + 3 bands), maximum 21.
- **Half-point steps.** Raw goes in 0.5 steps, so the table has **43 rows**, not 22. Our own sums are whole numbers (a band is an
  integer); the half points stay because the official table has them. `rawToSpeaking75` accepts 0 to 21 in 0.5 steps and returns null
  for anything else (it never rounds or interpolates).

| raw | 0–75 | raw | 0–75 | raw | 0–75 | raw | 0–75 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 5.5 | 27 | 11 | 43 | 16.5 | 59 |
| 0.5 | 10 | 6 | 29 | 11.5 | 45 | 17 | 61 |
| 1 | 11 | 6.5 | 30 | 12 | 46 | 17.5 | 63 |
| 1.5 | 13 | 7 | 32 | 12.5 | 47 | 18 | 64 |
| 2 | 15 | 7.5 | 33 | 13 | 49 | 18.5 | 65 |
| 2.5 | 17 | 8 | 35 | 13.5 | 50 | 19 | 67 |
| 3 | 19 | 8.5 | 37 | 14 | 51 | 19.5 | 69 |
| 3.5 | 21 | 9 | 38 | 14.5 | 52 | 20 | 71 |
| 4 | 23 | 9.5 | 39 | 15 | 54 | 20.5 | 73 |
| 4.5 | 24 | 10 | 40 | 15.5 | 56 | 21 | 75 |
| 5 | 26 | 10.5 | 42 | 16 | 57 | | |

- **Level** comes from the 0–75 score by the official bands (C1 65–75, B2 51–64, B1 38–50, below B1 0–37).
- **Where it shows:** only the teacher's mock view (the app's score, and "Your marks" from the teacher's own part marks when every part
  is rated). **Students never see the number** (owner decision 2026-11-15, see below): the full mock result, a single part's result,
  History and the dashboard show the level only. The table still decides the student's level. The line "Speaking only. An estimate from this mock, not an official
  UZBMB score." stays: the marks are our AI's, not an examiner's.
- **The interim rule (section 5) is replaced** for complete mocks (`estimateMockSpeaking` in `mock-score.ts`). `estimateSpeakingLevel`
  stays only as a fallback where no score can be computed (Part 1.1 has no band): it gives a level, never a /75 number.
- **Effect to know:** the official bands are stricter than the interim rule. Band 3 on Parts 1.1, 1.2 and 2 and band 4 on Part 3
  (raw 13) is **49 / 75, B1** (the interim rule said B2). B2 starts at raw 14.
- **Still not official:** the certificate level averages four skills; this is speaking only.

### Update 2026-11-15: the student sees the level, not the /75 number

The owner did not like "Speaking: 49 / 75" on the student's mock result, and it is removed there. Reasons: the level is what a student
plans around (B1 → B2), and a precise number built from our AI's practice bands reads more official than it is, which works against "a
score you can trust". The table, `rawToSpeaking75`, `levelOfSpeaking75` and `estimateMockSpeaking().score` all stay: the level still
comes from the official table and bands, and teachers still see the number on their mock view. To bring it back for students later,
restore the `score` field on `LevelTile` in `features/mock-result/view-model.ts`.
