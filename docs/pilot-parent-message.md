# Pilot: message to a parent

**Draft, 2026-11-08.** For the pilot only (about 10 known testers, nobody under 14). Asadbek sends it by message (or
prints it), fills in `[...]`, and keeps the parent's reply. Then he records consent with the owner script
(`docs/consent-design.md`, section 2b). Points marked **[CHECK]** need a lawyer. The Uzbek and Russian texts need a
native speaker's check before sending. Teacher view is off in the pilot, so the message asks nothing about teachers.
**The app does not delete audio after 30 days yet**: before sending, either that is built, or the owner deletes
pilot audio by hand (an owner script) at least every 30 days; otherwise remove that sentence.

## O'zbekcha

Assalomu alaykum!

Men Asadbek, CEFRO ilovasini yaratuvchisiman. Farzandingiz [ism] ilovani sinab ko'rishga taklif qilindi.

**CEFRO nima:** UZBMB multilevel imtihonining gapirish (speaking) qismiga tayyorlanish uchun bepul ilova.
Farzandingiz savollarga ovoz chiqarib javob beradi, sun'iy intellekt javobni baholaydi va nimani tuzatish kerakligini
aytadi.

**Nima saqlanadi:** ovozli yozuvlar, ularning matni (transkript), natijalar (ball va izohlar) va kirish uchun
elektron pochta manzili.

**Kim ko'radi:** faqat farzandingiz va men. Yozuvlar baholash uchun sun'iy intellekt xizmatiga ([provayder])
yuboriladi. Ma'lumotlar O'zbekistondan tashqaridagi serverlarda saqlanadi [CHECK]. O'qituvchilar hech narsani
ko'rmaydi.

**Qancha saqlanadi:** ovozli yozuvlar baholangandan keyin 30 kun o'tib o'chiriladi [CHECK]. Matn va natijalar akkaunt
o'chirilguncha saqlanadi.

**Qanday o'chiriladi:** farzandingiz istalgan vaqtda ilovadagi "Account" sahifasida "Delete my account" orqali
hamma narsani o'chirishi mumkin. Yoki menga yozing, men barcha ma'lumotlarni o'chiraman. Roziligingizni istalgan
vaqtda qaytarib olishingiz mumkin.

Agar rozi bo'lsangiz, iltimos, shu xabarga **"ha"** deb javob yozing va ismingizni hamda farzandingizga kim
bo'lishingizni (ona, ota yoki vasiy) yozing [CHECK: shunday javob yetarlimi].

Rahmat!

## English

Hello!

I'm Asadbek, and I made the CEFRO app. Your child [name] has been invited to test it.

**What CEFRO is:** a free app for practising the speaking part of the UZBMB multilevel exam. Your child answers
questions out loud; AI scores the answer and says what to fix.

**What is kept:** voice recordings, their text (transcript), results (scores and feedback), and an email address to
sign in.

**Who sees it:** only your child and me. Recordings are sent to an AI service ([provider]) to be scored. The data is
stored on servers outside Uzbekistan [CHECK]. Teachers see nothing.

**How long:** voice recordings are deleted 30 days after scoring [CHECK]. Text and results are kept until the account
is deleted.

**How to delete it:** your child can delete everything at any time on the app's Account page ("Delete my account").
Or message me and I will delete all the data. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

If you agree, please reply **"yes"** to this message with your name and your relationship to the child (mother,
father or guardian) [CHECK: whether such a reply is enough].

Thank you!
Asadbek, [phone or Telegram]
