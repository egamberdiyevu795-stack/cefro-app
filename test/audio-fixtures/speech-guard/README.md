# Speech Activity Guard fixtures

Small audio files used by `src/server/audio/speech-guard.test.ts`. **Every file is synthetic**:

- speech = the `espeak-ng` formant synthesiser (a robotic voice) reading short English texts, including deliberate
  learner mistakes ("Yesterday I have went to the market and I buy two bread...") and hesitations ("Um, well, ... uh ...");
- silence, noise, hum, clicks, a cough-like burst and keyboard-like taps = generated by ffmpeg filters;
- everything is encoded as mono Opus in WebM (what Chrome's MediaRecorder produces) with ffmpeg, plus one m4a (AAC) and
  two ogg (Opus) files for the other containers.

They are NOT recordings of a person. They exercise the signal logic only. Recorded speech from a real microphone
(developer-recorded or student-recorded) is a separate validation that has not been done.

Regenerate with `scripts/make-speech-guard-fixtures.sh` (needs `espeak-ng` and `ffmpeg`).
